Privacy policy

Health Export · Last updated 29 October 2026

Health Export is a small personal Android app (package au.u51.healthexport) published by U51. It copies your own health data from Health Connect on your phone into CSV files in your own Google Drive. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with that data.

What the app reads

With your permission, the app reads these Health Connect records from the last 30 days on your phone: steps, exercise sessions, sleep, weight and heart rate.

What the app does with it

Once a day, the app writes that data as CSV files to a folder in your own Google Drive. Processing happens on your phone. No data is sent to any server run by U51 or anyone else, apart from Google Drive itself.

Google Drive access

The app uses the drive.file scope, so it can only see and change files it created. It cannot read any other files in your Drive.

Health Export's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring your data to third parties.
  • No use of your data for any purpose other than writing the CSV files you asked for.

Storage and deletion

Your data stays in Health Connect on your phone and in the CSV files in your Google Drive, both of which you control. You can delete the files from Drive at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its local settings.

Revoking access

  • Google Drive: Google Account > Security > Third-party access (or myaccount.google.com/connections), then remove Health Export.
  • Health Connect: Settings > Health Connect > App permissions on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
