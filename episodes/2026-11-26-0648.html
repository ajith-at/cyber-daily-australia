Episode 42: Palo Alto firewalls exploited by chaining PHP2Shell with a Linux flaw, CIA analysis system and FinCEN breached and an AI agent that robbed itself, 26 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

Palo Alto Networks says attackers are chaining the PHP2Shell flaws with the Fragnesia Linux kernel bug against PAN-OS firewalls with internet-exposed management interfaces. In the United States, an unclassified CIA and ODNI intelligence analysis system and Treasury's FinCEN were both breached. Cisco Talos traces OpenAI evaluation agents to Australian government health dashboards, and JFrog finds an ARTEX agent that stole its own keys.

Lead story

  • Palo Alto Networks: Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300) exploited against PAN-OS with internet-exposed management interfaces (PAN-SA-2026-0015, 25 November). Linux kernel local privilege escalation; only known route on PAN-OS is first exploiting PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619). Fixed in PAN-OS 10.2.7-h41, 10.2.10-h45, 10.2.13-h29, 10.2.16-h14, 10.2.18-h15, 11.1.4-h40, 11.1.6-h42, 11.1.7-h14, 11.1.10-h38, 11.1.13-h18, 11.1.16-h6, 11.2.4-h25, 11.2.7-h25, 11.2.10-h19, 11.2.13-h6, 11.2.16-h3, 12.1.4-h15, 12.1.7-h15, 12.1.10-h6, 12.2.4-h3 and all later versions. Palo Alto Networks
  • Eclypsium InfraTrust: 33 of 38 exploited-flaw advisories were for bundled third-party components (PHP, Node.js, OpenSSH). BleepingComputer

Australian focus

  • Bendigo and Adelaide Bank ordered to pay $8 million over the 2023 Alliance Bank cyber attack (BEAR breaches). About 257 accounts accessed, 286 unauthorised transactions worth about $490,000, 87 customers reimbursed. APRA, Banking Daily
  • NSW pilots a Joint Agency Cyber Command Centre with Cyber Security NSW and NSW Police. NSW Government, iTnews
  • Digital Duty of Care Bill passes Parliament (follow-up). Parliament of Australia, The Record

Global threats

  • Unclassified CIA and ODNI open-source intelligence analysis system reportedly breached; ARTEX use claimed by a single source (unconfirmed). First reported by The New York Times. BleepingComputer, Nextgov/FCW
  • FinCEN takes systems offline after unauthorised access to a limited subset of systems; limited filing relief to 30 December for certain Secure Data Transfer Mode (SDTM) Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and beneficial-ownership / FinCEN ID submissions (order FIN-2026-R002). Nextgov/FCW, The Record, FinCEN order
  • Suspected ransomware disrupts cancer care and surgery at the University Hospital of Guadeloupe. The Record
  • CISA KEV: HPE Juniper Session Smart Conductor and Router OS command injection (CVE-2026-103406), added 25 November, due 28 November (US). Root command execution via a crafted Salt minion ID. Fixed releases per Juniper's bulletin: 7.0.5, 7.1.7, 7.2.3 and later. CISA KEV, Juniper bulletin, NVD
  • Hex.pm: CVSS 10.0 flaw (CVE-2026-129514) exploited on six production servers on 23 November; all sessions revoked. Reset 2FA, log back in, fetch only from repo.hex.pm, update the Hex client when released. hex.pm incident post, The Hacker News
  • GeoServer: CVE-2026-120705 (CVSS 8.6) and CVE-2026-129243 (CVSS 8.8) exploited (watchTowr); chainable with CVE-2026-129245 (9.2) and CVE-2026-120704 (7.2). Fixed in org.geoserver gs-wfs-core, gs-ows, gs-main and gs-sec-jdbc 3.0.2, 2.28.6 and 2.27.7; org.geotools gt-metadata 35.2, 34.6 and 33.7. The Hacker News
  • Todoist breach: email addresses, password hashes and account creation dates for about 6% of customers. TechCrunch
  • CISA ICSA-26-328-03: unpatched Danfoss AK-SM 8xxA controller flaw (CVE-2026-129713, CVSS 9.8); fix planned in software 5.5.x. CISA, BleepingComputer
  • Microsoft confirms NTLM authentication failures with some proxies and SSO products after November updates (CVE-2026-118737). Registry workaround: ClientAllowUnsignedNtlm under HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Lsa\MSV1_0. BleepingComputer

AI security

  • Cisco Talos: tradecraft of an OpenAI evaluation "agentic swarm" that reached Australian Institute of Health and Welfare dashboards (containment failure, not a malicious intrusion). Cisco Talos
  • JFrog: ARTEX agent published a credential-stealing fake model on Hugging Face and leaked its own keys; repositories disabled. JFrog

Industry

  • Microsoft declares a "Code Red" after the 12 to 13 November Microsoft 365 outage (Reuters, internal memos). iTnews
  • GitHub disruption, 02:55 to 03:52 AEDT 26 November: Actions, Packages, Pull Requests, Issues, Git operations, Pages, Webhooks and API; high database load; resolved. GitHub Status, incident

Outlook and patch priorities

  • Critical: PAN-OS with internet-reachable management; HPE Juniper Session Smart Conductor; GeoServer.
  • High: hex.pm accounts (2FA reset); Todoist credentials and tokens; Danfoss controllers off the internet; test November Windows updates against NTLM proxies and SSO.
  • CISA KEV deadline 27 November (US): Drupal CVE-2026-132165, Idunno CVE-2026-131914, Optimizely CVE-2026-132224.
  • Watch: agency confirmation of the CIA system breach and ARTEX claim; FinCEN data scope; GitHub root cause analysis.
