Episode 3: ConnectWise backup credential abuse and Drupal critical patches, 18 October 2026

An attacker used a stolen ConnectWise Cloud Backup (formerly SkyKick) credential to sign in to at least 162 Microsoft 365 tenants, and ConnectWise has shut the service while customers disable the apps and hunt for planted credentials. Drupal shipped fixes for a critical remote code execution flaw in core and warned exploits may follow quickly. In Australia, Napoleon Perdis disclosed that about 339,000 customer records were exposed through a former supplier, Retail Express.

Reporting window: 24 hours to 7:00 am AEDT, Sunday 18 October 2026. Open sources only.

Headlines

  • ConnectWise Cloud Backup (SkyKick) credential abused against Microsoft 365 tenants: app-only sign-ins on 15 October bypassed MFA; Huntress confirms 162 tenants (173 per later reporting); in one tenant a Cloud Management app made itself Global Administrator and added secrets to other trusted apps; Microsoft calls it an active supply chain attack. Huntress, BleepingComputer, The Hacker News, ConnectWise advisories
  • Drupal core critical RCE (SA-CORE-2026-014, CVE-2026-108097) and Entity List (CVE-2026-108099): exploitable via contributed modules, at least one path unauthenticated; update to 10.6.19, 11.3.19 or 11.4.10 and Entity List 3.0.10, or uninstall field_ui. Drupal, BleepingComputer
  • CISA KEV deadlines: no new additions this window; Atlassian Data Center (CVE-2026-21589) due 18 October, BeyondTrust Remote Support (CVE-2026-102764) and Fortinet (CVE-2025-61624) due 19 October. CISA KEV
