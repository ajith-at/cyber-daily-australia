Episode 35: NGINX HTTP/2 code execution flaws, Microsoft's AI agent package trojanised on PyPI and Optimizely's Australian victims named, 19 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

Researchers have disclosed HTTP/2 flaws in the NGINX web server, rated critical by Wiz, that can allow unauthenticated remote code execution, with fixes released. Two malicious releases of Microsoft's agent-framework package on PyPI carried a credential stealer aimed at Azure and CI pipelines. Optimizely has published the root cause of its Web Experimentation hijack, and Australian sites including Vodafone, HSBC Australia and Rest Super are among those affected, while Java4Shell fixes are out and US officials say a recent wave of outages was not a cyberattack.

Lead story

  • NGINX HTTP/2 flaws: CVE-2026-123007 and CVE-2026-123633 (rated critical by Wiz; NGINX rates them major and medium). Memory corruption in HTTP/2 flow control (vulnerable 1.9.5 to 1.31.8) and a use-after-free in HTTP/2 (vulnerable 1.19.7 to 1.31.8), reachable without authentication through standard TLS processing; Wiz built lab proof-of-concept exploits; no exploitation reported. Fixed in NGINX OSS 1.30.7 (stable) and 1.31.9 (mainline); NGINX Plus 37.0.8.1 and 37.3.0.1 (CVE-2026-123007), 37.0.7.1 and 37.2.0.1 (CVE-2026-123633). The same release fixes eight more issues, including CVE-2026-123634 (resolver), CVE-2026-121810 (HTTP/3), CVE-2026-118899 (TLS 1.3 request body loop) and CVE-2026-113425 (mail proxy). Interim mitigation: disable HTTP/2; restart after upgrading. nginx security advisories · Wiz · Rapid7

Australian focus

  • Optimizely root cause and Australian sites affected. Optimizely's incident report says an attacker exploited a flaw in the Web Experimentation account signup flow to act as administrator users (not a credential breach), editing Project JavaScript from 07:51 UTC on 16 Nov; flaw closed 16:10 UTC, code removed 22:24 UTC. Strong CSP blocked the skimmer; hosted payment iframes (Stripe, Adyen) were not reachable. Report URI's list includes Vodafone, HSBC Australia, Rest Super, TPG Telecom, Betashares and Compare Club; TPG Telecom paused Optimizely and found no evidence of compromise of Vodafone systems or customer data. About US$7,040 traced to attacker wallets. iOS 18.7.3 and 26.3 or later are patched against the delivered exploit kits. iTnews · Optimizely incident report (PDF)

Global threats

  • Java4Shell (CVE-2026-114938): fixes released, CISA urges immediate patching. Oracle's November update and OpenJDK fixes shipped 17 Nov; CISA advises inventorying all Java runtimes including bundled copies, internet-facing first. No exploitation reported. CISA · The Record
  • Apache Roller CVE-2026-129448 exploited. Unauthenticated XML-RPC deserialisation RCE in Roller 4.0.0 through 6.1.4 (and 6.1.5 under CVE-2026-82384); upgrade to 6.1.6 or later. BleepingComputer
  • RIKEN takes Fugaku offline after unauthorised access. Access 10 to 13 Nov via a public operating-status dashboard; up to 4,597 Fugaku and RIKYU users' names, emails, IDs and stored SSH keys or credentials may be exposed; no evidence research data was taken. RIKEN · BleepingComputer
  • n0n lists Visa, Mastercard, JPMorgan Chase among 59 claimed victims. Visa says the incident began at a third-party provider in Asia Pacific, its systems were not involved and no card numbers appear affected; JPMorgan attributes its disruption to a software bug. Claims unverified. Cybernews · TechRadar · Ransomnews
  • Osaki Electric halts production after ransomware attack (Tokyo Stock Exchange disclosure, 18 Nov). Ransomnews register
  • The Gentlemen lists Nvidia on its leak site; no evidence published, no breach confirmed. Newsweek

AI security

  • Microsoft agent-framework trojanised on PyPI. Versions 1.26.1 (20:35 UTC, 17 Nov) and 1.26.2 (21:08 UTC) added a .pth credential stealer (environment secrets, credential files, /proc environ, Azure managed-identity and Azure DevOps OIDC tokens; 1.26.2 adds GitHub Actions OIDC) exfiltrating to webhook.site. Both removed; 1.26.0 is clean. Wiz reports a likely compromised Microsoft ESRP publishing certificate and tooling identifying as Cairn (not confirmed by Microsoft). Indicators: agent_framework_meta.pth, af_runtime_diag.pth. Rotate all reachable credentials. GitHub advisory GHSA-764m-3ppc-5m73 · OSV PYSEC-2026-4388 · Wiz · Endor Labs
  • Microsoft announces MAI Cyber 2 Flash at Ignite. Low-cost model for finding, confirming and fixing vulnerabilities, initially through Microsoft security products. Microsoft AI
  • OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber and Calif report about 35 Apple flaws, including a Lockdown Mode bypass (Forbes report; Apple has fixed many). Forbes México

Industry and policy

  • US officials: recent outages not coordinated or state-sponsored. DNI Jay Clayton told the House Homeland Security Committee there is no indication the incidents (flight ground stop, Outlook outage, NSANet, JPMorgan, United, Apple) are related or malicious; officials cite upgrades and patching. CNBC · The Washington Post · The White House

Outlook and patch priorities

  1. NGINX (including bundled copies and ingress controllers): upgrade to 1.30.7 or 1.31.9 (Plus builds above) and restart; else disable HTTP/2. Critical.
  2. agent-framework: hunt for 1.26.1 and 1.26.2 and the .pth files above; rotate credentials. Critical where installed.
  3. Java (CVE-2026-114938): apply November Oracle and OpenJDK fixes, internet-facing first. High.
  4. Apache Roller: upgrade to 6.1.6 or later. High where deployed.
  5. CISA KEV deadlines: 24 Nov (Outlook CVE-2026-124194, Cisco BroadWorks CVE-2026-20320, Openwave Webtop CVE-2026-111793, Chromium CVE-2026-125350); 1 Dec (Puppet CVE-2026-115731).
