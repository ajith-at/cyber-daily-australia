Episode 39: GL.iNet routers and KVMs hit through cloud flaw, prompts stolen inside AI hosting providers and FBI crime database reportedly hacked, 23 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

GL.iNet says attackers abused its cloud remote management service to plant malware on customers' routers and KVM devices, and owners need to check, clean and update. Researchers found prompts, including Zero Data Retention requests, stolen from inside three AI inference providers for months, and OpenRouter has dropped three providers. The FBI is reportedly investigating a hack of its National Crime Information Center, while Cleverbridge and Rackspace Spot disclosed breaches affecting payment cards and Kubernetes Secrets.

Weekend recap

  • Saturday, Episode 37: Cisco Expressway flaw exploited for root, AI agents find a zero-day for a suspected China-linked operator and USDA systems offline after a breach. Episode notes
  • Sunday, Episode 38, Sunday Special: AI Agents Hacking at Machine Speed. Opened with the exploited Microsoft Entra Connect Health flaw. Five takeaways: take management and research tools off the internet and require authentication; treat a patch release as a race measured in days; remove the easy wins (default credentials, unauthenticated databases and inference endpoints, unretired keys, missing MFA); automate first response on high-confidence detections; hunt for the noise and published framework indicators. Episode notes

Lead story

  • GL.iNet cloud remote management flaw exploited to infect routers and KVM devices. Cloud flaw fixed and path blocked (notice of 20 November); existing infections remain. Check for the process crond -i /tmp/.log. Stable security firmware for routers and KVMs scheduled for 22 November. KVM firmware (GL.iNet upgrade guidance, updated 22 November UTC): 1.10.3 or later (RM10, RM1, RM1V2, RM1PE, RM4PE), 1.10.3 beta or later (RM10RC), 1.8.7 or later (RMQ1). The security notice lists the first beta fixes, 1.10.2 beta (1.8.6 beta for RMQ1). Routers: latest security firmware for your model. Community findings (not confirmed by GL.iNet): installer ni.sh with payloads gl-helper and gb; reverse shells to 170.39.226[.]219; infrastructure 66.245.219[.]252 and 65.20.71[.]131; possible command injection in the GoodCloud AstroWarp/SD-WAN handler; 152 of 183 routers at one consultancy showed suspicious activity; some files date to December 2025. GL.iNet · GL.iNet forum guidance · BleepingComputer

Global threats

  • FBI reportedly investigating a hack of the National Crime Information Center (NCIC). First reported by CNN; FBI has not commented; entry point and data accessed unknown. AP · BleepingComputer
  • Cleverbridge says stored payment card data was copied. Detected 4 November, notices from 20 November; notices differ on whether CVV/CVC or contact details were copied; not ransomware; merchants not named. BleepingComputer · CyberSIXT
  • Rackspace Spot breach exposed customers' Kubernetes Secrets. Leaked credentials, 17 November about 00:20 to 22:00 UTC; Rackspace announced service fully restored 21 November. Indicators: 23.224.79[.]106, 23.224.0[.]0/15, pod and object prefixes i1, glm-, probe. Reissue Spot API and refresh tokens, fetch new kubeconfigs, rotate all Secrets, review downstream logs from 17 November. BleepingComputer
  • Ghostscript sandbox escape via crafted uploads (CVE-2026-125396). Use-after-free in font loading disables -dSAFER. Affected: Ghostscript before 10.08.0. Fixed: 10.08.0 (8 September 2026). WordPress closed its own upload path in 7.0.4 (CVE-2026-65640). Debian 12 listed vulnerable, Amazon Linux fix pending, SUSE fixes in progress (as of last week). Update bundled and container copies too. No exploitation reported. The Hacker News
  • GPGME double free via malicious OpenPGP keys (CVE-2026-129975). Affected: GPGME before 2.3.0 (2.3.0 never publicly released), shared, DLL or statically linked. Fixed: GPGME 2.3.1; Gpg4win 5.1.2; legacy branch 1.24.4. GPGMEPP and GPGMEQT need no update. No attacks reported. The Hacker News
  • Palo Alto Networks: NGINX HTTP/2 flaws in PAN-OS and Panorama (CVE-2026-123633, CVE-2026-123007). CVSS-B 9.2, critical; suggested urgency moderate (high attack complexity). Only affects HTTP/2-enabled configurations; unauthenticated worker crash or code execution. Fixed now: 12.2.4-h3, 12.1.10-h6, 11.2.16-h3. Due 23 November (US time): 12.1.7-h15, 11.2.13-h6, 11.2.10-h19. Not affected: 12.1 below 12.1.5 and 11.2 below 11.2.8. Workaround: disable HTTP/2. No exploitation known. Palo Alto Networks
  • CISA KEV deadline. Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466, VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890, SAP NetWeaver CVE-2025-42884 and Apache Roller CVE-2026-129448 are due 23 November (US time). No new additions since 20 November. CISA KEV

AI security

  • Prompts stolen inside three AI inference providers; OpenRouter drops three providers. Dragos canary research by HD Moore (nearly two million seeded prompts, July to November); Zero Data Retention-flagged requests copied; 125 endpoints removed from Zero Data Retention eligibility; ARTEX used to validate stolen keys on 1 November; ARTEX user agents artex-agent and artex-judge-r121. Phala, Together AI and Novita disclosures; Together says an AI coding agent committed a Hugging Face credential to its public GitHub repository (xorl), used to download stored prompt samples, and five credentials the attacker tested were Dragos canaries. Dragos · OpenRouter · Together AI · The Hacker News
  • Meta Muse agent hijacked in a lab demonstration (fixed). Browser flaw, outbound monitor misclassifying requests, then a container escape to the user's VM. Fixes are NebuSec's account; Meta has not commented publicly. NebuSec · The Hacker News
  • Signal restricts screenshots by default to block OS AI agents on iOS 27 and Android. Signal calls it incomplete. The Hacker News

Australia

  • Barefoot Investor says scammers cloned his voice with AI. Scott Pape says an AI voice clone impersonated him on Facebook to tout fake investments; no victim numbers established. His advice: authenticator app instead of SMS codes, password manager, family code word. news.com.au

Outlook and patch priorities

  • Critical: GL.iNet routers and KVMs (check, update, reset if infected, inspect KVM-connected computers); Rackspace Spot credential rotation.
  • High: Ghostscript in upload, preview and print pipelines (including bundled copies); GPGME and applications that bundle it; PAN-OS and Panorama with HTTP/2 enabled; KEV items due today.
  • Watch: GL.iNet root cause and scope; FBI statement on NCIC; names of breached AI providers; Cleverbridge merchants.
