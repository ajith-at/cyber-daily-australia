Episode 33: Coles investigates a cyber incident, Google says AI agent frameworks are running intrusions and the NoisyTCP flaw can crash Linux servers, 17 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

Coles Group is investigating a cyber security incident and says it has found no evidence to date that customer, team or supplier data was compromised or removed. Google Threat Intelligence Group reports attackers running intrusions with open-source AI agent frameworks and automating exploits from patches, and an 11-year-old Linux kernel flaw, NoisyTCP, can crash exposed servers. Microsoft has explained the root cause of last week's global Microsoft 365 outage.

Australian focus

  • Coles Group investigating a cyber security incident. No evidence to date that customer, team or supplier data was compromised or removed; working with the ACSC and outside experts to contain and secure its IT environment; stores and online running normally, some Liquorland click-and-collect issues. Coles has not said what was affected, how access was gained, whether ransomware was involved or when it began. Beware Coles-themed scam texts and emails. Unverified Reddit posts claiming to be from staff say some in-store stock tools are offline; Coles has not confirmed this (low confidence). Qilin's 15 Nov listing is Cole Manufacturing (US), not Coles (ransomware.live). ASX release · Coles updates · ABC · iTnews

Global threats

  • NoisyTCP (CVE-2026-99288): pre-auth remote crash in the Linux kernel TCP stack. Race in established-socket lookups with SYN cookies; affects kernels since 4.4 (2015); Red Hat CVSS 7.5. Fixed in stable kernels 6.12.117, 6.18.63 and 7.3.4 (upstream fix commit 4e2b3ef19e0e). Triggerable on hosts with two or more RX queues or RPS spreading packets across CPUs (common on VMs and modern servers). Workarounds: net.ipv4.tcp_syncookies=0 in each network namespace, or a single RX queue with RPS/RFS disabled. No exploitation reported. Researcher advisory · oss-security · Kernel CVE announcement · iTnews
  • Microsoft 365 outage root cause (preliminary report). Latent bug in a July networking gateway update, triggered by Teams failover traffic plus about 14% capacity already offline; more than 80% of gateway servers unresponsive at worst; Exchange Online could not fail over. Final report expected this week. BleepingComputer
  • Lithuanian Customs partially restores services after cyberattack. BAP user portal shut after the 10 Nov detection, disrupting EU customs systems including ICS2; possible breach of users who logged in 9 to 10 Nov. The Record · Lithuanian Customs
  • EU sanctions 30 individuals and two entities over Russian disinformation and cyber activity. Includes the Social Design Agency and 24 staff, and NoName057(16) (listed under the separate EU cyber sanctions regime). Council of the EU · The Record
  • Fandango checkout skimmer, 8 to 11 October. Card number, CVV, expiry, billing ZIP and email exposed; no count given. BleepingComputer · California AG notice
  • Uber: about 150,000 people affected via a compromised service provider, reported by TechCrunch as Fireworks AI. Names, addresses, driver's licence numbers. SecurityWeek · TechCrunch

AI security

  • GTIG AI Threat Tracker: agentic offensive frameworks and automated patch diffing. Artex, Cairn, CyberStrikeAI and PentAGI used in intrusions; LLM pipelines for patch diffing to weaponise n-days; after PaperCut NG/MF zero-day exploitation, GreyNoise saw a likely AI-built exploit campaign within four days of disclosure; LLMjacking resale; coding assistant configuration tampering. Google Cloud blog · The Hacker News
  • Barracuda: Artex-driven intrusion via an unrevoked third-party AI vendor key. About 10 cloud accounts in about 44 hours on 1 to 2 Nov; Kubernetes backdoor removed. Barracuda blog · SecurityWeek · AWS Security Blog

Industry and patches

  • Project Zero moves to "Full X" disclosure. Full 90 now (no 30-day patch soak), Full 60 from 2 Feb 2027, Full 30 from 3 Jan 2028. Project Zero · SecurityWeek
  • Windows Server 2016 end of support in about 60 days. BleepingComputer

Patch priorities

  • Linux kernels for NoisyTCP (fixed: 6.12.117, 6.18.63, 7.3.4, or your distribution's backport), then reboot.
  • Revoke unused third-party cloud access keys; alert on use of old keys.
  • CISA KEV due 24 Nov 2026: Microsoft Outlook (CVE-2026-124194), Cisco BroadWorks (CVE-2026-20320), Openwave Webtop Media Transcoder (CVE-2026-111793), Google Chromium (CVE-2026-125350). CISA KEV
