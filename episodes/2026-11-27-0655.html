Episode 43: 1Password backend breach via GitHub, Flight Centre booking data may have been accessed and Mitel MiCollab zero-days exploited, 27 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

1Password says an attacker used a hijacked employee GitHub account to push malicious code to its backend, copy customer account records and source code, and reach its build pipeline, while vaults were not affected. Flight Centre is investigating access to a third-party accommodation booking system, and Mitel MiCollab flaws were exploited as zero-days before a patch existed. Switzerland confirms its first successful AI-agent cyberattacks as Microsoft names six actors using the ARTEX framework.

Lead story

  • 1Password: backend compromised through an employee's corporate GitHub account; account records, private source code and CI/CD access taken; vaults, vault keys and recovery keys not affected. Investigation began 19 November. Records include names, emails, account and user IDs, sign-in times, public keys, SRP parameters and tokens from some invitation, provisioning and recovery emails. Credentials rotated; email codes lengthened from six digits to ten characters. No evidence of malicious changes to apps or extensions. Watch for phishing about invitations and account recovery. 1Password, The Hacker News

Australian focus

  • Flight Centre Travel Group: "significant number" of customer records may have been accessed in a third-party accommodation booking system (ASX, 26 November). Affects the Flight Centre brand, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout and some independent agents. Mostly names and basic travel details; a very small percentage with limited passport details, contact details, dates of birth or health and mobility information. No card or bank details accessed to date; flight and corporate bookings not affected. Mandiant engaged. ASX statement, iTnews

Global threats

  • Mitel MiCollab AWV zero-days exploited: CVE-2026-133323 (CVSS 10.0), CVE-2026-133303 (9.0), CVE-2026-133311 (5.9), CVE-2026-133275 (5.3), CVE-2026-133324 (5.3); added to CISA KEV 25 November. Affected: MiCollab 10.3 (10.3.0.18) and earlier. Fixed: upgrade to 10.3.0.18 and apply the patch in KB000129195, or upgrade to a later release. Mitigation: restrict external access to the AWV component. Accenture APEX Labs saw web shell activity before Mitel's 16 November advisory, root within 30 minutes and domain controller access within 32 minutes; suspected (not confirmed) use of two commercial AI API services. IoCs: 204.76.203[.]204, 204.76.203[.]216; web shell path /pictures/*/shell.asp?c= (shell.jsp also tried). Mitel MISA-2026-0012, Accenture APEX Labs, SecurityWeek
  • Vates Xen Orchestra authentication bypass exploited (CVE-2026-133469, VSA-2026-062; CISA KEV, due 28 November). Empty-object login token gives admin rights; no log of successful exploitation. Fixed in Xen Orchestra 6.9.2 (stable) and 6.10.1 (latest); xo-server 5.211.2 (stable) and 5.213.1 (latest). IoCs (from the advisory): 91.211.251[.]112:8001, 193.148.56[.]72, 37.221.208[.]216, 45.66.35[.]11, 64.65.0[.]120, 64.65.62[.]160, 89.47.51[.]90, 92.60.37[.]247 and a Tor onion service; request pattern authenticationToken=j:{} or authenticationToken=j:%7B%7D; a "pivot2" VM; /etc/cron.d/zz-ustar*, /.ustar5, /.ustar6. Vates
  • Danfoss AK-SM 800A: CVE-2023-25914, CVE-2023-25915 (KEV due 28 November) and CVE-2025-41450 (due 9 December) added to CISA KEV. See Danfoss advisories DSA-2023-08-01 and DSA-2025-03-01. CISA KEV
  • Drupal SA-CORE-2026-019 (CVE-2026-136716): highly critical installer access bypass exploited in the wild (follow-up). Affected: below 10.6.23; 11.0.0 to below 11.3.23; 11.4.0 to below 11.4.14. Fixed: 10.6.23, 11.3.23 and 11.4.14, then run update.php. Drupal 11.2.x and below and 10.5.x and below are end of life. Check logs for changes to or by user 1. Drupal
  • PolinRider loader force-pushed into open pull requests at AsyncAPI Generator, CircuitVerse and Open Food Facts; CircuitVerse release CI ran it and 40 installers were deleted. Loader found in 576 of 622 repositories checked. Command server 95.135.181[.]84 (as of 25 November). North Korea link is press and vendor attribution. StepSecurity, The Hacker News
  • NFS2ROOT (CVE-2026-99362): pre-authentication heap overflow in the Linux kernel NFS server (NFSv4.1/4.2, TCP 2049). Present since Linux 3.16; exploit private; no exploitation reported. Fixed in 6.12.117, 6.18.61, 7.3.2 and 7.4-rc1; check distribution backports. OpenSec, Linux kernel CVE announcement, Red Hat
  • FIFA confirms unauthorised access to a "limited set of personal and business information"; Handsome Boy Hacking Club claims 12 GB (unverified). The Record
  • Zodia Custody: limited client and employee business contact details accessed; no unauthorised transactions. Zodia Custody, CoinDesk

AI security

  • Microsoft: Storm-0940, Storm-3358, Onyx Sleet, Pearl Sleet, Crystal Typhoon and Salt Typhoon have integrated the ARTEX agentic framework. No new techniques, but machine speed; privileged access in under three hours in multiple Storm-0940 intrusions; behavioural detection advised. Microsoft Threat Intelligence, The Record
  • Switzerland's NCSC confirms first successful AI-agent cyberattacks, including critical infrastructure operators; CSIRT Italia reports parallel agent campaigns. Swiss advice: patch critical internet-facing systems within 24 hours. Case counts (five confirmed cases, three aimed at critical infrastructure, two successful) are from a media interview. Swiss NCSC, swissinfo.ch, BleepingComputer

Industry

  • UK National Cyber Action Plan delayed again, now expected 9 December (anonymous sources). The Record
  • US: ransomware recovery firm CEO indicted, accused of secretly paying ransoms while claiming to have decryption tools (allegations). The Record

Late item

  • Late item: EasyEquities (South Africa) restricts client logins after a security incident. Owned by JSE-listed Purple Group; client funds "accounted for and secure"; still assessing whether personal information was accessed; follows a September breach at third-party identity verification provider RelyComply. EasyEquities, TechCentral

Outlook and patch priorities

  • Critical: Mitel MiCollab (patch, restrict AWV, hunt for prior compromise); Xen Orchestra (6.9.2 or 6.10.1); Drupal (10.6.23, 11.3.23 or 11.4.14, then update.php).
  • High: Danfoss AK-SM 800A; Linux hosts serving NFSv4.1/4.2; CI workflows that run fork pull requests.
  • CISA KEV deadlines (US): 27 November: Drupal CVE-2026-132165, Idunno AntiCsrf CVE-2026-131914, Optimizely CVE-2026-132224. 28 November: Mitel (four), Vates CVE-2026-133469, Danfoss CVE-2023-25914 and CVE-2023-25915, HPE Juniper CVE-2026-103406. 9 December: Mitel CVE-2026-133311, Danfoss CVE-2025-41450.
  • Watch: phishing themed on 1Password invitations or recovery and on Flight Centre bookings.
