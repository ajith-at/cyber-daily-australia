Episode 41: Check Point servers hacked in 17 countries, ASX uranium miner halts trading after cyber incident and another critical vCenter flaw, 25 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

An actor that GreyNoise believes leans on AI tooling compromised Check Point Security Management servers at 22 organisations in 17 countries, including Australia. ASX-listed Peninsula Energy halted trading after a cyber incident at its Wyoming uranium mine, and Broadcom fixed another critical vCenter authentication bypass plus ESX code execution flaws. The UK NCSC reports a marked rise in AI-assisted attacks that exploit long-standing weaknesses.

Lead story

  • GT13 compromised 24 Check Point Security Management servers at 22 organisations in 17 countries and territories, including Australia. Activity seen 17 October to 2 November 2026 (visibility then ended; likely ongoing). Post-compromise exploitation of CVE-2026-16232 (SmartConsole auth bypass; fixed in Jumbo Hotfix R82.10 Take 36, R82 Take 118, R81.20 Take 158; published and added to CISA KEV 22 July); Rejetto HFS relay via CVE-2026-61500; firewall databases, credentials, configuration and internal CA files stolen; policies unloaded; bulk certificate operation on 26 October. GreyNoise assesses LLM and agentic tooling use (not independently confirmed); no attribution. Indicators include the domain newpayapp[.]com and subdomains data-zt.newpayapp[.]com and api-zt.newpayapp[.]com; full list in the GreyNoise report. GreyNoise · Check Point sk185169 · BleepingComputer

Australian focus

  • Peninsula Energy (ASX: PEN) halts trading after a cyber incident at its Lance uranium project, Wyoming. Incident late on Friday night Perth time (AWST), 20 November; "the breach was to the Company's operating system only"; US and Australian administrative systems not affected; operations temporarily affected; halt until normal trading on 25 November or an announcement. ASX trading halt · SecurityWeek
  • Silverstripe CMS fixes two critical flaws (SS-2026-008, CVSS 9.8; SS-2026-009, CVSS 9.1). Unauthenticated remote template loading via PHP allow_url_fopen (on by default); sign-in to any passwordless account, including the default admin. No CVEs yet. CMS 6 sites also need silverstripe/template-engine 1.0.3; versions older than 5.4 are patched best-effort only (upgrade advised); see Silverstripe's security releases for the exact fixed versions per framework line. Interim: disable allow_url_fopen, restrict /admin and /Security, audit passwordless accounts. iTnews · Silverstripe
  • Snowy's Camping Goods: malicious code on its website, 5 October to 16 November 2026. Checkout details and, for sign-ins and registrations, email addresses and passwords targeted; no indication card details involved; affected passwords reset. Based on the company's customer notice as reported. YourLifeChoices
  • Digital Duty of Care Bill: Labor and Greens deal to pass the Senate. Adds a private right of action against social media companies and raises the maximum penalty to up to 5 per cent of global revenue (from $109.2 million). Senate agreed to the second reading on 24 November and began Committee of the Whole; not yet passed by the Senate, and Senate amendments need House agreement. ParlInfo · ABC News

Global threats

  • Broadcom VMSA-2026-0011: 23 vulnerabilities in ESX, vCenter, Workstation, Fusion, VMware Tools and Avi Load Balancer. CVE-2026-123903 (CVSS 9.8) VMware Directory Service authentication bypass, distinct from exploited CVE-2026-123890; ESX flaws allow code execution by a network attacker; also CVE-2026-123904 (7.5) and CVE-2026-123905 to CVE-2026-123907 (7.5). No exploitation reported. vCenter 8.0 U3m (from VMSA-2026-0009) already resolves vCenter 8.x; vCenter 9.1.x (9.1.2), 9.0.x (9.0.2 express patch), ESX and VMware Tools need this advisory's updates. See the VMSA for exact fixed versions. Broadcom VMSA FAQ · The Hacker News
  • Radware: unauthenticated internal Cloud WAF management API exposed (19 November). Customer contact details and, for a subset, log-export credentials (AWS S3, Azure Blob Storage, SFTP) potentially accessed; rotate and review storage logs since 19 November. Based on a root cause report seen by BleepingComputer. BleepingComputer
  • ShinyHunters claims return; Okta links vishing cluster O-UNC-053 to the new INCEPTION leak site. BleepingComputer · Okta
  • Backdoor in six WP connect WordPress plugins (CVE-2026-135202, CVSS 9.8). Air WP Sync 2.9.1 to 2.9.4, WP Sync for Notion 1.7.4, Add-on Brevo for Gravity Forms 2.6.3, Add-On for Contact Form 7 to Airtable 2.5.4, Add-On for Contact Form 7 to Notion 1.6.4, Orders Sync to Airtable for WooCommerce 1.0.2. No patch: uninstall and scan. Wordfence
  • WHMCS: unauthenticated SQL injection (CVE-2026-82981, CVSS 8.7) plus two authenticated flaws including CVE-2026-93700 (9.0). Fixed in 9.0.10 and 8.13.8; advisory names 9.1.2 for the 9.1 series; 8.0 to 8.12 unsupported. For CVE-2026-93700, also delete planned or ongoing email campaigns and clear the template cache per the advisory. No attacks reported. The Hacker News
  • KEPCO breach exposes data on more than 1.5 million people. BleepingComputer
  • McDonald's Japan app database breach (purchase history, IP addresses, device location). BleepingComputer
  • Drupal SA-CORE-2026-017 (CVE-2026-132165) exploited since 19 November 19:00 UTC; added to CISA KEV on 24 November (due 27 November) with Optimizely (CVE-2026-132224) and Idunno.AntiCsrf (CVE-2026-131914); MONAI Label (CVE-2026-133968) and N-able Mail Assure (CVE-2026-134624) added 23 November (due 26 November). Drupal affected: below 10.6.22; 11.0.0 to below 11.3.22; 11.4.0 to below 11.4.13. Fixed: 10.6.22, 11.3.22, 11.4.13. MONAI Label: 0.8.5 and earlier affected, fixed in 0.8.6. Drupal · CISA KEV

AI security

  • UK NCSC: marked rise in AI-assisted attacks since September 2026 using agentic offensive frameworks against long-standing weaknesses. NCSC · The Record
  • OpenAI and Cisco release Shirudo, an open-source eBPF runtime for agent-written, operator-approved exploit shields. OpenAI · Cisco

  • High: WHMCS update and campaign clean-up; Radware Cloud WAF customers rotate log-export credentials.
  • Watch: Peninsula Energy ASX announcement; exploit code for VMSA-2026-0011 and Silverstripe; CISA KEV deadline 26 November (US) for MONAI Label and N-able Mail Assure.
