Episode 44: US Air Force cloud platform reportedly breached, SAP confirms a security incident and an unpatched Cacti zero-day, 28 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

The US Air Force's Cloud One platform has reportedly been breached through an unidentified software flaw, and SAP has confirmed a security incident amid insider reports that stolen employee tokens were used to copy and alter source code on GitHub. A public exploit for an unpatched Cacti flaw gives unauthenticated command execution, while the UK Department for Work and Pensions and AFP took services offline after cyber incidents.

Lead story

  • US Air Force Cloud One enterprise cloud platform reportedly breached (Reuters). Intrusion found within the past month during a platform upgrade; an unidentified software vulnerability reportedly exploited; attacker, scope and data theft unknown; US Cyber Command assessing. Air Force declined to comment. Anonymous sourcing. BleepingComputer

Global threats

  • SAP confirms a security incident. SAP: suspicious activity identified, no evidence to date of unauthorised access to customer business data. Der Spiegel, citing an internal memo, reports the code name "Project Granite"; insiders say stolen employee authentication tokens were used to copy and modify source code on GitHub, possibly with AI tools; not confirmed by SAP. BleepingComputer
  • Cacti zero-day "4HTTP2SHELL": unauthenticated RCE in four HTTP requests, public PoC, no fix yet. Stored SQL injection in guest users' graph display settings alters the real-time poller's commands. Requires the guest account to be enabled (disabled by default since Cacti 0.8.7). Tested on Cacti Core 1.2.31; earlier versions unconfirmed. Mitigate: disable guest access, restrict the web interface. BleepingComputer, The Hacker News
  • UK Department for Work and Pensions takes some online services offline after a cyber incident. Benefit and pension payments unaffected; working with the NCSC and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre. GOV.UK statement, The Record
  • AFP takes its AFP Forum client platform offline after an overnight intrusion (26 November, Paris time). Unavailable until further notice; attribution and data access unknown. The Record
  • Microsoft Store app "Setup Assistant" (ETPS Ltd) delivers the new Vinci RAT (eSentire). Chain starts at a fake snipping-tool site serving a Microsoft-signed installer. IoCs: snipp.codes-team[.]com, setup-assistant[.]com, monkeysholder[.]info. The Hacker News
  • Trojanised Q-Dir and DesktopOK installers on the official SoftwareOK site deliver a Python RAT (AhnLab). Confirmed poisoned: Q-Dir 12.85.0.0, 12.84.0.0 and 12.83.0.0; DesktopOK 12.85.0.0 and 12.82.0.0. IoC: mail.c1a2[.]top. AhnLab ASEC, The Hacker News
  • WeaveLotus: suspected Vietnam-linked espionage against Thailand, the Philippines and Indonesia (SOCRadar). Access via GeoServer, JasperReports, osTicket flaws and exposed Laravel debug mode; attribution is the vendor's assessment. The Hacker News
  • Japan's education ministry (MEXT) discloses procurement system breach. Company names, representatives' names and other business details (up to 163,953 records) and up to 1,778 email addresses potentially exposed; detected 1 October; no sign of publication or misuse. MEXT notice, The Record
  • CISA KEV additions, 27 November (follow-up): GeoServer CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129243; Drupal core CVE-2026-136716 (SA-CORE-2026-019). US federal due date 30 November. CISA KEV

AI security

  • SAP incident: insiders say attackers possibly used AI tools; unconfirmed by SAP. BleepingComputer

Industry

  • Windows Server 2028 removes the WSUS server role. WSUS stays supported on earlier releases, including Windows Server 2025, for their lifecycles. BleepingComputer
  • Out-of-band hotpatch KB5138318 fixes Windows Server 2025 stop error 0x139 after the November hotpatch KB5131077. Affects some servers with Router Advertisement enabled receiving time-bound IPv6 routes; installs automatically, no restart. BleepingComputer

Outlook and patch priorities

  • Critical: Cacti: disable the guest account, restrict web access, review poller configuration; watch for a vendor fix.
  • High: GeoServer and Drupal (CISA KEV); remove the listed Q-Dir and DesktopOK versions and reinstall clean copies; hunt for "Setup Assistant" by ETPS Ltd.
  • Watch: SAP disclosure on affected code; Cacti exploitation in the wild; DWP and US Air Force updates.
