Episode 37: Cisco Expressway flaw exploited for root, AI agents find a zero-day for a suspected China-linked operator and USDA systems offline after a breach, 21 November 2026

SpotifyApple PodcastsPocket Casts

Attackers are exploiting a critical Cisco Expressway flaw for root access and lateral movement, and CISA has added it to its exploited list alongside SAP NetWeaver, VMware vCenter and Apache Roller. Amazon details a suspected China-linked operator whose Cairn AI agents found and exploited a zero-day in MONAI Label at East Asian universities. The US Department of Agriculture has taken systems offline after a security incident, and Australia's Bell Financial says client data was likely accessed.

Lead story

  • Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466 exploited (CVSS 9.8), added to CISA KEV (due 23 November). Persistent cookies and unvalidated client certificates in the clustering database service allow unauthenticated root command execution. Fixed: X15.5.3 and earlier, upgrade to X15.5.20; X14 and earlier, migrate to a fixed release. No workaround. Cisco's advisory has not been updated to reflect exploitation. F6 reports two internet-facing devices and two internal cluster nodes compromised, then credential theft via custom Python scripts. Indicators (F6): 66.179.31[.]32, 18.130.47[.]116; "wombat@127.0.0.1" in erlang.log; scripts in /mnt/harddisk/i{0-9}{4}[a-zA-Z]?/*.py. Cisco advisory · F6 · The Hacker News
  • Other CISA KEV additions (20 November US time, due 23 November). SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal CVE-2025-42884 (JNDI injection); VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890; Apache Roller CVE-2026-129448 (broader version range than CVE-2026-82384). CISA KEV

Australian focus

  • Bell Financial says some client data likely accessed. Unauthorised access to a limited number of IT systems, now contained; ACSC and AFP notified, OAIC engaged. iTnews · ASX release · Bell Direct

Global threats

  • USDA takes applications offline after security incident. Database passwords reset across the Farm Production and Conservation Business Center's environments (internal email); farmer payment and loan tools disrupted; actor and data impact unknown. Nextgov/FCW
  • N-able confirms breach behind Mail Assure and SpamExperts outage. One hosted cloud server accessed; Americas, Asia-Pacific and Europe affected; no evidence of mailbox access; stored credentials may have been exposed, so customer API tokens were revoked. BleepingComputer · SecurityWeek
  • Wellington IT breach hits 40+ Irish credit unions. Payee details in mobile banking apps accessed; six-week notification delay. The Record
  • Transport for London: about 265,000 Oyster photocard customers' details exposed. BleepingComputer
  • Linux KVM CVE-2026-99312 on AMD hosts with nested virtualisation. Guest read about 127 GB of host memory; fixed upstream; update host kernels or disable nested virtualisation. The Hacker News
  • Unpatched Windows "FastUnlock" lock screen bypass. Physical access, limited non-admin account; no fix yet. The Hacker News

AI security

  • Amazon: Chinese-nexus actor's Cairn AI agents found a zero-day. Exposed Cairn console revealed attacks on East Asian universities; agents exploited a path traversal in MONAI Label to gain root. Advisory GHSA-qc22-rmgc-vg94: monailabel 0.8.5 and earlier affected; check the advisory for the fix. AWS Security Blog · MONAI Label advisory · BleepingComputer
  • Bell Canada says an "agentic" threat actor stole enterprise customer data. Pre-production circuit provisioning system accessed on 17 November; method not disclosed. BleepingComputer

Industry and policy

  • National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross departs; Kirsten Davies acting. The Record
  • SonicWall: retire Generation 6 firewalls now. No further patches, even for critical flaws. The Hacker News
  • F5 BIG-IP out-of-band hotfixes for five critical APM and iRules flaws. CVE-2026-121805, CVE-2026-120292, CVE-2026-120285, CVE-2026-116953, CVE-2026-123637, plus high-severity CVE-2026-126822. Affected and fixed versions are listed per CVE in F5's articles. BleepingComputer · F5 K000163937 · F5 K000163939 · F5 K000163940 · F5 K000163941 · F5 K000163942 · F5 K000163946
  • Drupal core installer RCE (CVE-2026-132165, SA-CORE-2026-017). Affected: before 10.6.22, 11.0.0 to before 11.3.22, 11.4.0 to before 11.4.13. Fixed: 10.6.22, 11.3.22, 11.4.13. Block /core/install.php. Drupal.org

Outlook and patch priorities

  • Critical: Cisco Expressway (upgrade, hunt with the indicators above, rotate reachable credentials); SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal, vCenter and Apache Roller (KEV, due 23 November).
  • High: F5 BIG-IP APM and iRules; Drupal core; AMD KVM hosts with nested virtualisation.
  • Exposure: internet-facing MONAI Label and similar research tools; SonicWall Gen 6.
  • Watch: more Expressway victims; Bell Financial data scope; USDA disclosure.
